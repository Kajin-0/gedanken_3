# Rev. 4 layout polish — 2026-08-14

The first rendered PRA Rev. 4 passed scientific QA but still contained production-level sloppiness, most visibly in Figure 1.

## Figure 1 defects in the first render

- competing labels and arrows reduced hierarchy;
- the direct-bright-overlap annotation was crowded into the lower-left path;
- the gate condition looked like a disconnected mini-panel;
- raster embedding made the schematic typography softer than the surrounding REVTeX text;
- the causal/decision semantics were visually ambiguous.

## Corrections

Figure 1 was redrawn as a clean four-block schematic:

```text
signal photon -> bright state -> counted sink
                    |
                 dephasing
                    v
                dark manifold
                    |
              slow recycling
                    ^
```

The efficiency-selected gate is now explicitly labeled as a **decision condition**, not a physical downstream state. The accepted-dark functional is placed as the detector-level observable rather than mixed into an arrow path.

The new figure is generated by:

`numerics/paper_rev4_fig1_clean.py`

and is intended for vector PDF embedding in the manuscript.

## Additional document-level cleanup

A two-column orphan was also removed in Sec. IV. In the first render, the word `so` was stranded at the top of the right column between the definition of `q_N` and its large-N sector limit. The two equations are now one continuous aligned block introduced by:

> The fast branching fraction and its large-N limit are ...

This removes an obvious production artifact without changing the mathematics.

Figures 2 and 3 are also embedded as vector PDF rather than raster PNG in the polished local render.
