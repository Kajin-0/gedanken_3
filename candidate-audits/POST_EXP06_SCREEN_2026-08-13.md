# Candidate screen after Experiment 06

**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Purpose:** preserve early kills so future agents do not reopen occupied paths without a new premise.

## 1. Photon-momentum provenance — STOP

Photon drag and momentum transfer to semiconductor carriers are established, including ultrafast photon-drag detectors. The photon momentum is also small relative to ordinary carrier momentum and survives only on the momentum-relaxation timescale. Do not open a dark-event provenance project around this without a genuinely different observable.

## 2. Spin/valley provenance — STOP

Optical spin injection plus spin-selective carrier transport is already the operating principle of spin photodiodes and modern circular-polarization photodetectors. Do not relabel polarization-selective photodetection as dark-current rejection.

## 3. Joint charge + heat provenance — STOP

Cryogenic semiconductor detectors already combine primary phonon and ionization/Neganov-Luke measurements, resolve single e-h excitations, and use detector-response differences to study leakage, trapping, impact ionization and event-source backgrounds. This is an occupied detector class.

## 4. Random-alloy HgCdTe cutoff limit — STOP AS NOVELTY PATH

HgCdTe alloy disorder, compositional fluctuations, localized excitons and Urbach/band-tail broadening are established. A binomial random-alloy derivation would be a useful estimate but not a new detector principle, and real short-range order/annealing prevents a universal random-alloy lower-bound claim.

## 5. Discrete trap-count yield — USEFUL BUT NOT NEW

If active defects are independently distributed,

```math
N_t\sim Poisson(n_tV),
```

so relative device-to-device spread grows as `1/sqrt(n_t V)` and `P(N_t=0)=exp(-n_tV)`. Below `n_tV<ln2`, the median device is trap-free even though the ensemble mean trap current is nonzero. This is standard Poisson-yield/single-defect RTN physics; HgCdTe RTN from individual defect-related mechanisms is established.

## 6. Naive finite-volume suppression of Auger — REJECTED

For a homogeneous carrier gas with Poisson carrier counts, an r-body bulk mass-action reaction remains extensive. For electron-electron-hole Auger,

```math
a_A=(C/V^2)N_e(N_e-1)N_h,
```

and

```math
E[a_A]=C n^2p V.
```

Thus entering the `nV<1` regime does not by itself produce extra mean Auger suppression; events become intermittent. More generally, Poisson factorial moments restore the continuum mass-action mean. Quantum confinement can change matrix elements/phase space, but then the microscopic material problem has changed.

## 7. Coulomb-blockade/quantum-dot escape — STOP AS BROAD PREMISE

Quantum-dot infrared photodetection, Coulomb-blockade photodetection, single-photoelectron transistors and near-zero-dark-current quantum-dot/SET detectors are established. A new project would need a much narrower mechanism than 'use occupancy blockade to suppress dark carriers.'

## Current rule

Do not open Experiment 07 until a microscopic premise survives both:

1. an explicit strongest-architecture/prior-art screen;
2. a check that its proposed advantage is not merely a known side channel, mode filter, quantum-dot detector, or standard fluctuation-response result.
